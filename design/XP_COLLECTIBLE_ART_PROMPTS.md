# Schwung — XP collectible illustrations

Seven alpine objects, one per XP milestone (`src/core/reset/collection.ts`). Each needs a **dark** and a **light**
version with identical geometry. Prompts are standalone: paste one at a time.

## Delivery (all seven)

- **Format:** PNG, **1024 × 1024 px** (1:1), **transparent background** (true alpha, no white or coloured box,
  no drop shadow baked onto a background). The object is centred and fills about 70% of the canvas, with even
  padding so it sits well in a circle.
- **Two versions, same drawing:** generate the dark version first, then ask for "the identical object, same
  geometry, same pose and framing, re-lit for a light theme". Only lighting and palette change.
- **Files** (into `assets/collectibles/`, then listed in `src/components/collection/art.ts`):

| Collectible | XP | Dark | Light |
|---|---|---|---|
| The Compass | 60 | `assets/collectibles/compass-dark.png` | `assets/collectibles/compass-light.png` |
| Pressed Edelweiss | 180 | `assets/collectibles/edelweiss-dark.png` | `assets/collectibles/edelweiss-light.png` |
| The Trail Journal | 380 | `assets/collectibles/trail_journal-dark.png` | `assets/collectibles/trail_journal-light.png` |
| The Mountain Lantern | 700 | `assets/collectibles/mountain_lantern-dark.png` | `assets/collectibles/mountain_lantern-light.png` |
| The Climbing Rope | 1,100 | `assets/collectibles/climbing_rope-dark.png` | `assets/collectibles/climbing_rope-light.png` |
| The Walking Staff | 1,600 | `assets/collectibles/walking_staff-dark.png` | `assets/collectibles/walking_staff-light.png` |
| The Summit Pennant | 2,300 | `assets/collectibles/summit_pennant-dark.png` | `assets/collectibles/summit_pennant-light.png` |

Locked objects are drawn by the app as a silhouette of the same PNG, so a clean, readable outline matters.

## Shared style (included in every prompt below)

Quiet painterly digital art in the Schwung alpine world: soft brushwork with a fine film grain, gentle atmospheric
depth, muted alpine palette (deep indigo, slate blue-violet, misty blue, sage, warm stone, muted peach and a little
soft gold for light). Calm, timeless, handcrafted, like an object resting in early mountain light. Dark version:
lit by low dusk or blue-hour light against nothing (transparent), cool indigo shadows, warm small highlights.
Light version: the same object in soft overcast morning light, lavender-white mist tones, gentle shadows, never
washed out. No lettering, numbers, logos or symbols, no cartoon or vector-icon style, no photorealism, no neon,
no glossy 3D render, no game-badge frame, ribbon, shield or medal, no background scene, no cast floor shadow.
