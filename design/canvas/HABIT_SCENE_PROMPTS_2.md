## 1. Setup message

Send once, in a new chat. Attach the three approved pilots as style references:
`design/artwork-candidates/scene-mind_read-dark-v1.png`, `scene-focus_deepwork-dark-v1.png`,
`scene-move_walk-dark-v1.png`, plus `design/canvas/review/panorama-dark-v2-overview.jpg`.

```
I'm making the backgrounds of habit cards for my app, Schwung. The habit's name, today's target and a "swipe to
complete" bar are written over the lower part of each image.

Attached: three finished scenes (Read, Focused work, Walk) and the world they belong to, one continuous painted
mountain landscape. Every new scene must look like a fourth, fifth, sixth… scene from that same set, painted by the
same hand:
- the same quiet, painterly, atmospheric style and fine grain: not photographic, not cartoon illustration
- dark version: deep indigo twilight, violet-grey mist, a thin warm glow only along the far horizon
- light version (later, as a relight): pale morning mist, soft lavender-grey, airy alpine calm
- one simple subject, small in a big calm landscape; restrained colour; soft depth and mist; premium and calm

Composition rules for every scene:
- exactly 1536×1024, landscape
- the subject and focal point sit in the UPPER 40% of the frame
- the LOWER 40% is calm and even in tone (soft mist, meadow, water or shadow): no bright spots, no busy texture,
  nothing important (text sits there)
- the subject stays inside the central 80% of the width; nothing important in the bottom 7%

Never: text, letters, numbers, logos, screens showing anything, UI, faces or close-up figures, animals, loud
gradients, neon, lens flare, emoji-like or cartoon objects, gym or stock-photo looks, or anything that looks like
generic AI art.

I'll ask for eight scenes, one at a time, dark first. Reply with the image and the prompt you used.
```

## 2. The scenes (dark)

Send one at a time; wait for an image you like before the next.

### Sleep Routine (`sleep_wake`)

```
Scene: SLEEP ROUTINE (getting up on time). The very first light in the Schwung world.
A small canvas tent pitched on a quiet shelf of the mountainside, its front flap tied open, facing a ridge where
the first thin warm line of dawn is just appearing under the indigo sky. Inside the tent, only a soft dark
shape of a folded sleeping bag. Tent and dawn line sit in the upper part of the frame. The lower part is the dark,
smooth slope and low mist: calm and even. No person, no fire, no lantern.
```

### Get It Done (`focus_top3`)

```
Scene: GET IT DONE (today's few important tasks). A clear, simple plan in the Schwung world at twilight.
A small open notebook and a pencil on the plank table of a hut's porch railing, three short pen strokes on the
page with three small tick marks beside them (no readable writing), looking out over the valley to the far
ridges and the thin horizon glow. The notebook sits in the upper part of the frame, seen from a little below so the
view opens behind it. The lower part is the dark porch boards and soft shadow: calm and even.
```

### Less Scrolling (`screen_app_limit`)

```
Scene: LESS SCROLLING (putting the phone away). Attention given back to the world, at twilight.
A smartphone lying face-down on a flat stone at the edge of a still mountain lake, small in the frame, its back
plain and dark (no logo, no light from the screen); beyond it the lake mirrors the far ridges and the thin warm
glow, and the view is clearly the point. Stone and phone sit in the upper part of the frame, slightly off-centre.
The lower part is the still, dark water and low mist: calm and even.
```

### Strength Training (`strength_log`)

```
Scene: STRENGTH TRAINING. Quiet strength in the Schwung world at twilight.
Two plain iron kettlebells resting on a broad granite ledge high on the mountain, a rolled training mat beside
them, the ledge looking out over a sea of mist to the far ridges and the thin horizon glow. Heavy, still, grounded.
The kettlebells and ledge sit in the upper part of the frame. The lower part is the dark rock face falling into
mist: calm and even. No person, no gym equipment beyond these, no brand marks.
```

### Tidy Up (`home_tidy`)

```
Scene: TIDY UP. Order and calm in the Schwung world at twilight.
The small porch of a timber mountain hut, everything in its place: a pair of boots side by side, firewood
stacked neatly against the wall, a broom leaning by the door, one window glowing softly. Simple, uncluttered,
cared for. The porch sits in the upper part of the frame, slightly off-centre, with the valley and far ridges
beside it. The lower part is the dark, even ground in front of the hut with low mist: calm and even. No person.
```

### Practice a Skill (`skill_practice`)

```
Scene: PRACTICE A SKILL (any skill: an instrument, a language, a sport, a craft). Steady practice, step by step.
A line of rough stone steps climbing a gentle slope toward a small flat lookout, each step a little higher than
the last, the lookout catching the faint warm glow, with the far ridges beyond. Nothing on the steps that names a
particular skill. Steps and lookout lead the eye into the upper part of the frame. The lower part is the dark
meadow at the foot of the steps in low mist: calm and even. No person.
```

### Stay Connected (`social_reach_out`)

```
Scene: STAY CONNECTED (reaching out to people you care about). Two lights across the valley at twilight.
Two small timber huts on opposite sides of a misty valley, each with one warm window lit, a thin pale path
winding down from one and up to the other, so the two feel linked across the distance. Both huts sit in the upper
part of the frame, well apart, inside the central 80% of the width. The lower part is the valley floor filled
with soft, even mist: calm. No people, no wires, no signs.
```

### Social Confidence (`confidence_talk`)

```
Scene: SOCIAL CONFIDENCE (one small brave step toward people). Stepping across, at twilight.
A simple wooden footbridge over a narrow mountain stream, leading from the dark near bank toward a softly lit
lakeside camp on the far side, where a small warm fire glows and two or three tiny silhouettes stand far away
(no detail, no faces). The bridge leads the eye into the upper part of the frame, toward the light. The lower
part is the dark near bank and the stream in low mist: calm and even.
```
