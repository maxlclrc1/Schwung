# Schwung: app overview

**Schwung** (German for "momentum") is a free iPhone app for one program: the **66-day Life Reset**. There's no
account, no paywall, no ads and no tracking; everything stays on the phone and works offline.

**Pitch:** "Get your momentum back. Free. No account. No paywall."

## Screens and flow

1. **Start screen:** the painted mountain, "Get your momentum back.", and "Start my reset".
2. **Set-up** (about a minute):
   - **About you:** age (16+) and an optional health question.
   - **Habits:** two to six of the 11 (Focus Time, Less Scrolling, Get It Done, Read Daily, Social Confidence,
     Daily Movement, Sleep Routine, Tidy Up, Practice a Skill, Stay Connected, Strength Training), with a
     suggested three. Age, eligibility and health-precaution rules apply.
   - **Only the questions those habits need**, then an intensity for each (Starter, Standard, Challenge) and the
     time available, then a review. "Begin Day 1" starts the program.
3. **Habits tab:** each habit as an illustrated card with today's target; swipe a card right to complete it (left
   to undo). Weekly habits count by the week, with rest days never missed. Counters, strength sessions and
   readiness offers (a step up from Day 22 or 45, or a step easier) live here too. **Add a habit** (up to six)
   adds one of the 11 from today, asking only what it needs.
4. **Journey tab:** the trail of 66 days up the mountain, the stats (days done, streak, best), any day's habits,
   and a 66-day calendar.
5. **Insights tab:** consistency, the weekly trend, each habit's bar and history, a 66-day heatmap and personal
   records, from the saved days only.
6. **Settings:** the program's day and "End the Life Reset", appearance, export, privacy and contact.

The three tabs are one swipeable pager over a single painted world (dark and light), with the panorama moving
with the pages.

Rules, numbers and the reasons behind them: `SCHWUNG_LIFE_RESET_PROGRAM.md`.
