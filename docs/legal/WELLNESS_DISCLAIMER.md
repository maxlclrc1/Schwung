# Schwung Wellness Note

> Final wording for review; shown in the app at **Settings → About → Wellness note**. The in-app copy lives in
> `src/core/legal.ts` (`WELLNESS`): change both together. Not legally reviewed (see `LEGAL_REVIEW_CHECKLIST.md`).

Schwung is here to help you build everyday habits and look after your general wellbeing. It isn't a medical device,
and it doesn't diagnose, treat or prevent any condition.

## General guidance, not medical advice

Suggestions about movement, strength training, sleep, screen time and other habits are general guidance. They aren't
tailored medical, fitness or mental-health advice, even when Schwung adjusts them to your answers.

If you have a health condition, an injury, are pregnant, take medication, or aren't sure an activity is right for
you, talk to a doctor or another qualified professional before starting or changing it, and follow their advice over
Schwung's.

## Listen to your body

Stop and seek help if you feel pain, dizziness, chest discomfort, shortness of breath or anything that worries you.
Rest days and easier days are part of the program, never a failure.

## About the 66 days

The 66 days give the Life Reset its shape. Research suggests habits can take very different amounts of time to
settle, from a few weeks to many months, so the timing will be your own. Finishing the program doesn't guarantee a
habit is formed, and taking longer is normal.

## If you need support

Schwung can't help in an emergency. If you're in crisis or think you may be in danger, contact your local emergency
number or a crisis service near you.

Schwung is for people 16 and older.

---

**Unchanged safeguards in the app:** the under-16 stop at set-up, the optional health-precautions question
(skipping it is treated as "unknown", never as "no limitations"), gentler targets and "keep to what your clinician
has advised" notes for people with precautions, shorter focus blocks for 16–17-year-olds, and rest days that never
count as missed.
